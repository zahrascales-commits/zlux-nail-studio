Before You Join

How does it work at checkout?

Enter your Member ID when you book. Your service shows as $0.00 and you leave owing nothing. No deposit, ever.

Every 4 weeks or annually?

Both get you the same thing. Paying annually gets you three visits free.

Can I cancel?

Yes — from your own account, in two clicks. Memberships run three months minimum, then it is up to you. Nothing already paid for is refunded, and you keep everything until it ends.

What about pedicures?
