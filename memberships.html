Common Questions

Before You Join

How does it work at checkout?

Enter your Member ID when you book. Your service shows as $0.00 and you leave owing nothing. No deposit, ever.

How often can I come?

You choose: every 2, 3, 4 or 5 weeks, on any membership. Every 2 weeks saves you 10% on every visit, every 3 weeks saves 5%, every 4 weeks is the regular price, and every 5 weeks is $10 more. Your membership is paid on the same rhythm, so there is nothing to pay at the appointment. At every 4 weeks you can also pay for the year — that comes with 2 visits free.

Can I cancel?

Yes — from your own account, in two clicks. Memberships run three months minimum, then it is up to you. Nothing already paid for is refunded, and you keep everything until it ends.

What about pedicures?

A Russian pedicure is $75 on any visit, or $85 with the full correction — on either membership. Walk-in prices are $95 and $125.
