First To Confirm ✦

A booking with no artist chosen goes out to everyone who can actually take it — trained on that service, rostered in for those hours, nothing else booked. The first to confirm gets it, and only then does the client get their confirmation naming her.

Connect Texting 📱

📱 Text delivery: checking…

Paste the 3 values from your Twilio account (Console home page shows all three). Texting turns on the moment you save — no redeploy needed.

Connect Email ✉️

✉️ Email delivery: checking…

Paste your Resend API key (free at resend.com → API Keys → Create). Email turns on the moment you save.

Must be at a domain you verified in Resend. Leave this blank and mail goes out from Resend's test address, which only ever reaches you — clients get nothing.

Connect Payments 💳

💳 Payments: checking…

Paste your Stripe keys (Stripe Dashboard → Developers → API keys). Use your live keys to take real money. Payments turn on the moment you save — no redeploy.

Test keys let you practice with fake cards. Live keys take real payments into your bank. Your secret key is stored privately and never shown on the website.

Sets up the $99 / $199 / $299 monthly plans on your connected Stripe account. Run this once after connecting keys (and again if you switch to live keys).
