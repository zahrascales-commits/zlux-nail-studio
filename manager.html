A booking with no artist chosen goes out to everyone who can actually take it — trained on that service, rostered in for those hours, nothing else booked. The first to confirm gets it, and only then does the client get their confirmation naming her.

Paste your Stripe keys (Stripe Dashboard → Developers → API keys). Use your live keys to take real money. Payments turn on the moment you save — no redeploy.
