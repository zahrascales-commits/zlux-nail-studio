Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

ZOLA Nail Studio ("ZOLA", "we", "us") operates zolanailstudio.com and provides nail services in Porterville, California. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how our text message program works.

Text Message (SMS) Terms

What you're agreeing to

When you book an appointment and provide your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from ZOLA Nail Studio relating to that appointment — confirmations, reminders, schedule changes, and follow-ups.

Separately, and only if you tick the optional box at booking or sign up for our list, you may also receive marketing messages about new press-on drops, openings, offers, and studio news. Marketing messages are optional and are never required to book an appointment or receive a service.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Appointment messages are sent as needed around your bookings — typically 2 to 5 per appointment. Marketing messages, if you opted in, are sent no more than a few times per month.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. ZOLA does not charge for text messages, but your mobile carrier may.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to be removed immediately. You'll get one confirmation message and nothing after that. You can also reply HELP for assistance, or email us at zolastudioempire@gmail.com.

Opting out of marketing messages does not cancel your appointments, and you will still receive essential messages about a booking you have made unless you ask us to stop those too.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or your SMS consent with anyone. No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are used solely by ZOLA Nail Studio to contact you about your appointments and, where you have opted in, about our own offers.

What we collect

  • Contact details — your name, phone number and email address, given when you book, join our list, or contact us.
  • Appointment information — the services you book, dates and times, your artist, and any notes or inspiration photos you choose to send us.
  • Nail and service preferences — colours, shapes, lengths, sensitivities and press-on sizes, so we can prepare properly for your visit.
  • Payment information — card payments are processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Website usage — anonymous page view and scroll information that helps us see which pages are useful. This is not tied to your name.

How we use it

  • To book, confirm, remind you of, and deliver your appointments
  • To prepare for your visit — supplies, colours, and your saved preferences
  • To take payment and issue receipts
  • To send marketing messages and emails, only where you have opted in
  • To answer your questions and provide support

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary, and only with services that help us run the studio:

  • Stripe — to process payments securely
  • Twilio — to deliver text messages
  • Resend — to deliver email
  • Vercel and Turso — to host the website and store booking records

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. As stated above, mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared for marketing purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop texts — reply STOP to any message
  • Stop emails — reply to any email and ask, or use the unsubscribe note at the bottom
  • See your information — email us and we'll tell you what we hold
  • Delete your information — email us and we'll remove your records, other than what we must keep for tax and payment purposes

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of in that respect. Email us to exercise any of these rights.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. Guests under 18 are welcome in the studio with a parent or guardian, but we do not knowingly collect information online from children under 13.

Keeping your information safe

Access to client records is limited to Zahra and trained ZOLA team members. Payment details never touch our systems. We keep records only as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal obligations.
