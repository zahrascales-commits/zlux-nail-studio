Questions

Where is ZOLA Nail Studio?

We are at 2037 W Linda Vista Ave #D, Porterville, CA 93257. The studio is private and appointment-only, so there is no queue and no waiting room.

Do you take walk-ins?

No. Every appointment is booked in advance, which is how your time stays yours. Book online and pick the exact slot you want.

How far ahead do I need to book?

At least three days ahead. The calendar opens about four months out, and members book ahead of everyone else.

What does a set cost in Porterville?

A regular gel manicure is $55, Gel X and acrylic sets start at $95, and the Russian dry pedicure is $95. There are also $75 Tuesdays and $65 Wednesdays each week.

Do I have to be a member?

No. Anyone can book any service. A membership just means one full service every time you come, any design included, no deposit, and nothing to pay at the appointment.

What if I am coming from Lindsay, Strathmore or Visalia?

You are welcome — we are a short drive down the 65 or 190 from most of Tulare County, and appointments are set to the minute so the trip is worth it.
